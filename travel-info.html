Kyoto, Japan

Kyoto served as Japan's capital for over a thousand years and is home to more than 2,000 temples and shrines. The city is renowned for its classical Buddhist temples, stunning gardens, and traditional wooden houses.

  • Best Time: March – May
  • Language: Japanese
  • Currency: Yen (¥)
  • Must See: Fushimi Inari

Iceland

Iceland, the "Land of Fire and Ice," is known for its dramatic volcanic landscapes, geysers, hot springs, and glaciers. The northern lights paint the winter sky in shades of green, pink, and violet.

  • Best Time: June – Aug / Sep – Mar
  • Language: Icelandic
  • Currency: Króna (ISK)
  • Must See: Northern Lights

Bali, Indonesia

Bali is Indonesia's most famous island. Known as the "Island of the Gods," it offers lush rice terraces, ancient Hindu temples like Tanah Lot and Uluwatu, and world-class surf breaks.

  • Best Time: April – October
  • Language: Balinese / Indonesian
  • Currency: Rupiah (IDR)
  • Must See: Tegallalang Terraces

Swiss Alps

The Swiss Alps are one of the most iconic mountain ranges in the world. With snow-capped peaks, emerald valleys, pristine lakes, and charming alpine villages, Switzerland is a dream for nature lovers.

  • Best Time: June – Sep / Dec – Mar
  • Language: German / French / Italian
  • Currency: Swiss Franc (CHF)
  • Must See: Matterhorn

Paris, France

Paris, the "City of Light," is known for its art, fashion, gastronomy, and culture. The Eiffel Tower, Louvre Museum, and Notre-Dame Cathedral are just the beginning.

  • Best Time: April – June / Sep – Oct
  • Language: French
  • Currency: Euro (€)
  • Must See: Eiffel Tower

Santorini, Greece

Santorini is a stunning Greek island famous for its whitewashed buildings with blue domes, dramatic cliffs, and breathtaking sunsets over the caldera.

  • Best Time: April – October
  • Language: Greek
  • Currency: Euro (€)
  • Must See: Oia Sunset
